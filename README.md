# 💪 Fitness & Membership Management Dashboard

### 1. Project Title

💪 Fitness & Membership Management Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze fitness center performance, membership trends, member demographics, revenue, expenses, and membership plans. The dashboard also includes BMI and calorie calculation features for additional fitness-related analysis.

---

### 2. Short Description

The Fitness & Membership Management Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence solution designed to provide a comprehensive view of fitness center operations and member information.

The dashboard analyzes membership performance, revenue, expenses, member demographics, membership plans, active and expired memberships, and monthly membership trends. It also includes interactive BMI and calorie calculation features based on user inputs.

---

### 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was developed using the following tools and technologies:

- 📊 Power BI – Used for dashboard development, interactive reporting, and data visualization.
- 🧮 DAX – Used for calculated measures, KPIs, and fitness-related calculations.
- 🧹 Power Query – Used for data transformation and preparation.
- 📈 Data Analysis – Used to analyze membership, financial, and demographic patterns.
- 📊 Data Visualization – Used to present business and membership insights.
- 🎛️ Interactive Filters – Used for dynamic analysis across different dashboard sections.

---

### 4. Data Source

The dashboard uses a fitness center dataset containing information related to:

- Member information
- Membership plans
- Membership status
- Membership dates
- Member demographics
- Gender and age
- Trainer information
- Revenue
- Expenses
- Monthly memberships
- Fitness-related metrics

The data was analyzed and transformed to develop interactive dashboards and generate meaningful fitness center insights.

### 5. Features

The dashboard focuses on the following key areas:

### Business Problem

Fitness centers manage large amounts of information related to members, memberships, trainers, revenue, expenses, and customer demographics. Without an interactive reporting solution, it can be difficult to monitor membership performance, understand customer demographics, track financial performance, and identify membership trends.

Additionally, members may require basic fitness calculations such as BMI and calorie requirements to better understand their fitness needs.

---

### Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of this dashboard is to provide an interactive view of fitness center performance and member information, enabling users to monitor memberships, analyze financial performance, understand member demographics, and access basic fitness calculations through a centralized Power BI solution.

---

### Business Impact & Insights

The dashboard helps users:

- Monitor overall fitness center performance
- Track active and expired memberships
- Analyze membership plan distribution
- Monitor revenue and expenses
- Identify monthly membership trends
- Analyze member demographics
- Compare membership performance across different plans
- Access BMI and calorie-related calculations
- Explore member information through interactive filters
- Support data-driven fitness center management
